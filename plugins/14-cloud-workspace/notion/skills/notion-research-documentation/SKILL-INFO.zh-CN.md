## 原始说明

Synthesizes Notion workspace evidence into cited answers, comparisons, briefs, or reports. Use to investigate a question, connect findings across sources, or reconcile conflicting information, with external research when useful. Simple lookups, fetching a known page, or summarizing one supplied page do not need this workflow.
