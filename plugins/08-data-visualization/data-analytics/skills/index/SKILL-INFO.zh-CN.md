## 原始说明

Answer product and business questions with data and route data-related work to the right focused workflow. Use for requests involving data, metrics, trends, comparisons, drivers, KPIs, analysis, dashboards, reports, charts, tables, SQL, notebooks, spreadsheets, market sizing, data quality, reusable data context, data definitions, or working preferences, whether or not Data is at-mentioned. Dashboards can use uploaded spreadsheets, CSVs, or TSVs as source data without making the deliverable a spreadsheet. Do not use Data for general writing, editing, coding, or explanations that require none of these workflows.
